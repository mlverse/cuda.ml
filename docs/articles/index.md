# Articles

### Guides

- [Install and manage
  cuda.ml](https://mlverse.github.io/cuda.ml/articles/install-manage.md):

  Install a prebuilt or source-built backend, inspect and audit its
  cache, and prepare GPU or CPU-only deployments.

- [Use cuda.ml with
  tidymodels](https://mlverse.github.io/cuda.ml/articles/tidymodels.md):

  Fit cuda.ml models through parsnip, preprocess predictors with
  recipes, and distinguish portable model arguments from engine-specific
  controls.

- [Save and restore
  models](https://mlverse.github.io/cuda.ml/articles/model-persistence.md):

  Choose a persistence format and move fitted cuda.ml models between R
  sessions.

- [nvForest inference and
  deployment](https://mlverse.github.io/cuda.ml/articles/nvforest.md):

  Train random forests on a GPU, import tree ensembles, and deploy
  nvForest inference on a CPU or GPU.
